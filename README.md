# Power-BI-Project-Pizza-Sales-Analytics


📊 Sales Analytics Project | Pizza Sales Performance
I worked on a Sales Analytics project focused on understanding pizza sales performance and customer behavior, delivering insights that support business decision-making.

Project Objective: To identify sales trends, customer demand patterns, and product performance in order to help the business improve revenue focus and operational efficiency.

Data Preparation & ETL
•    Used MySQL database to source raw sales data
•    Performed ETL activities (data extraction, cleaning, and transformation)
•    Prepared structured datasets for accurate and reliable analysis

Key Business KPIs
•    Total Revenue
•    Total Orders
•    Average Order Value
•    Total Pizzas Sold
•    Average Pizzas per Order
These KPIs provided a clear overview of overall business performance.

Customer Behavior Analysis
•    Analyzed day-wise and month-wise trends
•    Identified declining periods and peak demand periods
•    Highlighted when customer activity is high vs low, aiding planning decisions

Product & Category Performance
•    % of sales revenue by pizza category and pizza size
•    Total pizzas sold by category
•    Identified top and bottom performing pizzas based on:
        o    Quantity sold
        o    Revenue generated
        o    Total orders

Business Impact
This analysis helps the business:
•    Promote high-performing pizzas
•    Identify low-performing pizzas for improvement or discontinuation
•    Make informed decisions on product strategy and inventory planning
🛠 Tools Used: MySQL | Power BI | DAX | ETL
Always motivated to transform data into actionable business insight
